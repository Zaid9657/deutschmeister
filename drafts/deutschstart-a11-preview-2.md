Hallo,

most beginner courses teach you to describe a picture before they teach you
to answer "Und was machen Sie?" — and that question comes up in the first
five minutes of meeting anyone here.

## Heute: say what you do

> **Ich bin** Studentin. — I am a student.
> **Ich arbeite** bei Siemens. — I work at Siemens.
> **Und Sie?** — And you?

Notice there is no "a" before the job: *Ich bin Studentin*, not *ich bin eine
Studentin*. German drops it. That one habit makes you sound immediately less
like a textbook.

Add "Und Sie?" and you have done the thing most beginners never practise:
handed the conversation back.
