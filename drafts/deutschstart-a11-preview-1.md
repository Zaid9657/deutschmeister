## Heute: the sentence you will use most

Two words carry most first conversations in Germany:

> **Ich heiße** Ana. — My name is Ana.
> **Ich komme aus** Marokko. — I come from Morocco.

Say both out loud once, with your own name and country. That is lesson 1,
and you just did the hard part of it.

Lesson 1 takes about 15 minutes: the words, a short dialogue, the rule, and
practice that tells you immediately whether you got it right.
