# SEO audit — `claude-seo` full pass (2026-08-24)

Run of the third-party [`claude-seo`](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo) skill pack (MIT,
pinned `v2.2.4`, 31 sub-skills / 18 sub-agents) against this repo and site. Bootstrap instructions
and the environment quirks are in [`docs/seo-routines/claude-seo.md`](../docs/seo-routines/claude-seo.md).

This is not one of the two scheduled Routines and it did not run their protocol. `drafts/geo-tracking.csv`
is untouched — a `claude-seo` pass is a different instrument with different queries, and writing into
that ledger would break the comparability rule 6 exists to protect.

---

## How this was measured, and what that does not cover

**Production was never crawled.** `deutsch-meister.de` (apex, `www`, and the `.netlify.app` host) is
blocked by this environment's proxy with `CONNECT tunnel failed, 403`. Separately, `claude-seo`'s own
`scripts/url_safety.py` hard-blocks `localhost` and every RFC1918 range with no override — a
deliberate SSRF guard — so its bundled Python fetchers had nothing to fetch either way.

Instead a **complete production build was made locally** and the skills were applied to the artifact.
Grammar content was extracted through the Supabase MCP (the REST host is proxy-blocked too), giving
`dist/` with all 92 pages. `scripts/serve-like-netlify.mjs` then served it under `netlify.toml`'s real
headers, redirects and rewrites, so findings about status codes and headers are measured, not inferred.

This is what `CLAUDE.md` asks for anyway — verify against `dist/`, never a source grep — and it is
*more* accurate than a localhost crawl would have been: a crawler pointed at `127.0.0.1` would have
flagged all ~83 absolute `https://deutsch-meister.de/…` canonicals as cross-domain.

**What this audit therefore cannot tell you:**

- **Nothing about actual search performance.** `deutsch-meister.de` is not a verified Search Console
  property (see `seo-CORRECTION-2026-08-24.md`), so there are no impressions, clicks, positions, CTR
  or CrUX field data. Every ranking-side statement here is inference from page structure.
- **Lighthouse numbers are loopback lab numbers** with Google Fonts blocked by the sandbox. Useful
  directionally (bundle size, render-blocking, LCP element); not presentable as production scores.
- **Production could still differ.** Netlify's real behaviour is emulated from its config, not observed.
- **Competitor pricing was not re-verified** — every vendor domain is still proxy-blocked, so
  `/vergleich/`'s "Stand: Mai 2026" stamp is untouched. Re-tested this session; still blocked.

## The headline: three false claims were shipping, and a guard had stopped working

This audit set out to find optimisation opportunities and mostly found **claims-integrity defects** —
in a repo whose entire `CLAUDE.md` culture is built to prevent exactly them. That is the finding.

**1. A deleted page's FAQ was answering for ~29 live URLs.** `dist/app.html` still carried the
`FAQPage` JSON-LD of the SPA homepage deleted on 2026-08-22. Netlify matches header rules on the
*request path*, so `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` scoped to `/app.html` never reached `/faq`, `/ueber-uns`,
`/dashboard`, `/level/*` and the rest, which are 200-rewritten to that file. One answer was flatly
false: *"Is DeutschMeister free?" → "Yes, DeutschMeister offers free access to German grammar lessons,
vocabulary training, listening exercises, and video podcasts"* — against `FREE_LEVEL_LABEL = 'A1.1'`.

**2. `/podcasts/` promised transcripts that do not exist — for the second time.**
`marketing.js:71` says *"transcripts are empty — never claim transcripts"*, and
`prerender-spa-routes.mjs:75` carries a comment forbidding it after the 2026-08-16 audit removed it
once. It came back because `check-built-html.mjs` compared its banned literal **case-sensitively**:
the ban listed `'full transcript'`, the page said `"Full transcripts"`. The guard had never fired.
The same sentence was in `podcast-feed.js`'s RSS description, which nothing scans, and in both
`llms.txt` files, which exist to be quoted verbatim by machines.

**3. `/reading/` published a count the repo had explicitly refused to publish.** `marketing.js`
rule 2 documents that reading totals are *deliberately absent* because three audits disagreed
(52/66/74) and the per-level figures were *"a smooth 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10 ramp that matches no
measurement"* — ending "re-count against `reading_lessons` before adding one here." The page shipped
that ramp, a "52 Total Lessons" tile, and the 52 in its meta description.

Re-counted this session. **The real distribution is flat: 8 per level, 10 at B2.2, 66 total.** So
A1.1 was advertised as 3 lessons against a real 8, and all three audited totals were wrong.

The same measurement settled the rest of the unguarded counts. Most were right — 453 rule
explanations, 673 worked examples, 672 exercises, 48 listening exercises, 480 dialogue lines and 945
sentences all check out exactly, and are now recorded constants rather than floating numbers. Two
were wrong: **1,982 vocabulary words against a real 1,935**, and the 52 above.

## Shipped (Class A)

Every fix restates facts already on the page or already in the data layer. Nothing new is asserted.

| Area | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Claims** | Stale `FAQPage` removed from the SPA shell; transcript claims removed from `/podcasts/`, the RSS feed and both `llms.txt` files; reading and vocabulary counts measured and sourced |
| **Guards** | Banned-literal check made case-insensitive and widened; three `llms.txt` guards; a title-drift guard; an answer-block guard; `check-built-html.mjs` extended from **16 pages to 93** |
| **Language** | Nine German pages (`/grammar/` + the 8 level hubs) declared `lang="en"`, `og:locale=en_US` and `inLanguage:"en"` with entirely German titles, H1s and descriptions |
| **Soft 404s** | `/leitfaden/*` and `/vergleich/*` wildcard rewrites removed — the same soft-404 already fixed for `/grammar/*` |
| **robots.txt** | 14 per-agent groups collapsed into one, so the `Disallow` lines actually bind (RFC 9309: only the most specific matching group applies) |
| **Head drift** | All six prerendered routes shipped one title and rendered another |
| **Schema** | `/grammar/` hub given `BreadcrumbList` + `ItemList`; `/speaking/` had none at all; `/analyze/` breadcrumb; USD→removed on a EUR product; `webFeed` pointed at an HTML page; duplicate FAQ question; `og:type` article on all 71 Article pages; `Article.image` 0→71; guide `dateModified` now `factsCheckedOn` instead of the build date |
| **GEO** | Definition-first `answer` block on all four Leitfäden |
| **Misc** | 69 slashless hrefs in the noscript fallback; Navbar's two `/grammar` links; `404.html`'s homepage canonical; IndexNow submitting the sitemap index instead of pages; `\|\| true` swallowing the whole build chain; `.webp` cache headers; non-descriptive "Start" link text |

**Two things worth calling out.**

The **built-HTML guard went from 16 pages to 93**. It hand-listed 16, leaving the 64 grammar topics,
8 level hubs and 3 comparison pages — the entire SEO core — unverified. It now discovers pages from
`dist/`, with a `REQUIRED` floor so it still fails loudly when a build step breaks (verified by
hiding a page) and `--spa-only` still tolerates the absent Astro half.

The **GEO fix** was chosen against `geo-weekly.md`'s evidence-ranked order. Fix type 1 (add a citable
figure) did not apply — the figures are there and sourced. Fix type 2 did: on `/leitfaden/telc-b1/`,
the highest-volume page on the site, the first pass-mark figure sat at **37% depth** behind a 53-word
lead containing no extractable fact. Now **4.9%**. The pattern already existed at scale — `topicSeo.js`
gives all 64 grammar pages a `snippetIntro`; the four commercially important pages were the ones
without it. Every figure was verified against its own guide before being written.

## Needs your decision

**The SPA shell ships on 22+ crawlable URLs with no `noindex` and no canonical.** `/faq`,
`/ueber-uns`, `/vocabulary`, `/intro`, `/video-library`, `/level/*`, `/reading/<level>`,
`/listening/<level>` and `/grammar/overview` all return byte-identical content: the same generic
title, the same `og:url = https://deutsch-meister.de/`, and no canonical (the prerender script strips
it from `app.html` deliberately). 22 are reachable from indexable pages — the shared footer links
`/faq` and `/ueber-uns` from all 87 Astro pages.

The mechanical fix is one line: `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` in `index.html`, stripped
for the six prerendered routes. **But it would make `/faq` and `/ueber-uns` explicitly noindex**, and
an FAQ page for a German-learning site plausibly should rank. That is a product call, not a
mechanical one, so it is not shipped. Either prerender them properly (the full three-place treatment)
or accept them as noindex utility pages and reconsider the footer links.

## Drafted, not shipped (Class B)

- **[`eeat-author-identity.md`](./eeat-author-identity.md)** — the largest E-E-A-T gap. There is zero
  `Person` schema anywhere; `author` is `Organization` on all 71 Article pages. And the single
  strongest signal the site owns — *"Gegründet von einem Arzt in Deutschland"* — appears on **one page
  of 92**. Closing this requires a real name, which only you can supply.
- **`/ueber-uns` and `/faq` have no crawlable HTML** while being footer-linked from 87 pages. Same
  gate: fixing it means asserting who the company is.
- **`/speaking/` renders a sign-in wall** to anonymous visitors — ~25 words of unique content on a
  URL sitting at priority 0.9 in the sitemap. Needs a production render to confirm, then real
  above-the-gate copy, as `/analyze/` and `/level-test/` have.
- **`Offer.priceValidUntil`** on `/pricing/` — asserts a pricing commitment through a date. Yours to pick.

## Rejected, with the rule

Recorded so the same advice does not resurface next cycle.

| Finding | Rule |
|---|---|
| Add learner counts, "trusted by N students", testimonials, star ratings, `AggregateRating`/`Review` | User-facing counts are **content** counts, measured, with provenance. There is no review data — emitting it would be fabricated |
| Put prices in guide/comparison copy to win price-intent snippets | `tests/claims.test.mjs`: no price literal in page source |
| Re-verify competitors / advance the "Stand: Mai 2026" stamp | Vendor domains proxy-blocked; an overstated competitor price is actionable under **§6 UWG** |

| Expand `Quiz` into Practice Problem markup | Deprecated Nov 2025, support removed Jan 2026 |

## Roadmap

1. **One head registry.** The title drift existed because `prerender-spa-routes.mjs` and
   `src/pages/*.jsx` each own a copy of the same six titles. `docs/medmeister-parity-roadmap.md:51`
   already identifies this. A shared `src/data/seoRoutes.js` closes it permanently; the guard added
   here only detects it.
2. **One `Organization` entity with an `@id`.** The org is declared five different ways across both
   build trees, none carrying `@id`, so engines see several similar-but-unequal organisations.
3. **The SPA carries a second, non-indexable grammar corpus.** `src/App.jsx` still routes
   `/grammar/:level/:topicSlug` to SPA components while Netlify serves those URLs from Astro — the
   same "two homepages, one dead" shape `CLAUDE.md` records for the deleted `LandingPage.jsx`.
4. **Accurate `lastmod` / `dateModified` for the grammar corpus.** All 64 topics currently claim the
   build date. Needs `updated_at` selected in `grammar.js`. The guides are already fixed.
5. **The "Modelltest" cluster** — ~3,690/mo combined at competition indices 9–15, the lowest-competition
   cluster measured, and close to the product. Own it deliberately or decide not to.
6. **`/vergleich/` has no freshness mechanism** while vendor domains stay blocked. Getting them
   allowlisted is a prerequisite, and stale-page citation eligibility degrades from around November.
7. **Prerendered SPA routes are thin** — 37–297 body words. `/analyze/` is the one worth fixing first;
   Sentence X-Ray is the genuinely unique asset and its page renders two example sentences.
