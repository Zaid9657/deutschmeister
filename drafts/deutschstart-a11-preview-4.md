## Heute: the two-word fix for word order

German puts the verb in position two. Always.

> **Heute** *arbeite* **ich** in Berlin. — Today I work in Berlin.
> **Morgen** *komme* **ich** um acht. — Tomorrow I come at eight.

Start a sentence with a time word and the verb jumps in front of the "ich".
Say those two aloud; that pattern runs through the rest of the course.
