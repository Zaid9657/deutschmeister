## Try this before you read on

> **Ich möchte einen Kaffee, bitte.** — I would like a coffee, please.
> **Was kostet das?** — What does that cost?

*möchte* is the polite one — *ich will* sounds blunt to German ears, closer
to "I demand" than "I'd like". Beginners who only learn *wollen* get heard
differently than they intend, in every shop, for months.

The third free lesson is the café. You order, you change your mind about one
item, you ask the price — and the speaking practice listens to you say it.
