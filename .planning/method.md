# How we work toward the north star

*Set up 2026-10-31 on the founder's instruction. Mirrors the Notion board "DeutschMeister — Shared
Board" (Claude's memory; the founder does not read it). If Notion is not connected, this file and
[lessons.md](lessons.md) are the memory; say so in the session.*

**North star:** 1,000,000 USD in DeutschMeister sales per month (after VAT and refunds, at EUR/USD
1.14 ≈ €877k a month). Source: [.claude/GOAL.md](../.claude/GOAL.md).

## The three rules of every stage

1. **Every stage starts with research and evidence and ends with a recorded lesson.** Evidence means
   a number read in this session from production, a primary source, or a buyer's own words. A
   stage is not closed until its lesson is on the board and in `lessons.md`.
2. **Run a small test before any big build.** One page, one post, one email batch, one paid offer
   to ten people. Build the system only after the test sold something or clearly failed.
3. **Send the founder one decision per message**, and state each newly locked decision to him in
   one line in chat, so he can veto it there.

## Every task

- Names, in one line, the sales number it moves (package sales, seat orders, revenue per visitor,
  refunds) and a rough size in money.
- Distribution and conversion come before polish. The simplest version that can sell ships first.
- Owner-only limits do not change: money, access/entitlement/quotas, legal, posting under the
  brand, security/auth/RLS/new public endpoints, customer-data deletion, `CLAUDE.md`
  (`docs/agents/PROTOCOL.md` § v5 addendum). Routines are never changed, paused or created without
  showing the founder the exact new text first.

## Board protocol

Update the board in the same step as any of these five events:
1. the founder makes, changes or reverses a decision;
2. something proves false or a lesson is learned;
3. a stage passes or a new one starts;
4. a deliverable is saved;
5. money, credits, tools or access change.

Before ending a reply, the board must be newer than the last commit that carries a decision or
lesson. Every session reads the board (Notion search "DeutschMeister — Shared Board") before any
business decision.
