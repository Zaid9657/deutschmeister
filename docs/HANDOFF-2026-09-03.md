### The bug fixed in PR #50 (grammar ExercisePlayer)

`astro-site/src/components/ExercisePlayer.jsx`. `FillBlank` and `MultipleChoice`
each keep local state, and the parent rendered them from a bare conditional with
**no `key`**. React reused the instance when one question followed another of the
same type, so `FillBlank` stayed `submitted`: input disabled, holding the previous
answer, marked wrong against a prompt never seen, correct answer spoiled — **and
no Check button and no Next button.** Nothing clickable.

- Reproduced in Chromium at **question 2 of 8** on `/grammar/a1.2/question-words/`,
  screenshotted, then fixed by keying both components on `exercise.id`.
- Also: the results screen's only button was "Practice again" — now the next topic
  is primary, and which action is primary follows the score at the 70% threshold
  `recordAttempt` already uses.
- Guard added: `tests/exercise-player.test.mjs`.
- Verified in the real Netlify production chunk, not just locally.

**Unresolved about this bug — do not overstate it:** 504 completions exist on
topics whose exercise order contains an adjacent fill-blank pair, which the model
says should stall. So the real-world blast radius is NOT established, and it does
**not** explain the retention cliff.

## 5. The biggest measured leak, still open

Of 167 users who ever did a grammar topic, **69 did exactly one and never
returned.** Of those 69:

- **35 did "A1.1 · Alphabet & Pronunciation"** — the course's first lesson. That
  is half of all one-and-done users and 21% of everyone who has ever done a lesson.
- 32 entered at topic 1 of some other level (Genitive B1.1, Dative A2.1, Konjunktiv II B2.1).
- Almost nobody quits mid-level. **They quit on the first lesson they are given.**

`alphabet-pronunciation` is **100% multiple choice**, so the PR #50 bug does *not*
explain it. Working hypothesis: it is the content. The alphabet is the least
motivating possible first lesson for an exam-driven audience. Ankommo's equivalent
opener is *der/die/das* with a live question on the marketing page.

**Question put to the owner, still unanswered:** reorder A1.1 so lesson 1 has an
immediate payoff / rebuild the alphabet lesson as a hook / route new users by exam

## 6. The decision that blocks everything: monetization

Nothing further should be built on the money side until this is settled.

**Current product:** free tier = A1.1 only (`src/config/freeTier.js`); Pro =
€9.99/mo or €79.99/yr for all 8 levels + higher AI limits (X-Ray 50/day, speaking
30/mo, writing 20/mo).

**The arithmetic that should drive the decision:**

| Price point | Sales/month for €10k | Visits needed @1% conversion |
|---|---|---|
| €11/mo subscription | **909 active subscribers** | ~130,000 |
| €99 one-time | 101 | ~10,000 |
| €299 one-time | 34 | ~3,400 |

At €11/month, €10k is **arithmetically out of reach** — it needs roughly 1,000×
current traffic. Also: CPC on `telc b1`/`dtz` is ~€1.90, so at 1% conversion a
customer costs ~€190 — **paid acquisition cannot work below ~€200 a sale.** The
price point decides whether any acquisition channel besides free organic exists.

**Options on the table (owner leans toward courses):**
- **A. Courses as the product** (~€29–49/level, ~€99 all four). Matches his
  instinct and the sequencing work below. Risk: a "German course" is the most
  commoditised thing in the market and the least differentiated part of our product.
- **B. Exam packs** (~€49–69 per exam: all mock sets + AI writing marked to that
  exam's criteria + AI speaking on its task types). Where the moat is; the buyer
  has a dated deadline. Requires closing the mock gap (4 papers vs his 42).
- **C. Ladder** — free tools → €4.99 Basis (courses, no AI) → €12.99 Pro (AI) →
  €49–69 Prüfungspaket. Subscription becomes the floor, not the product.
- **D. Other** — B2B seats to Sprachschulen/nurse recruiters; a €149 human
  writing-correction tier. Explicitly **not** his 1:1 services model (German
  liability).

**Important tension to flag honestly:** I recommended B (exam packs) *before*
seeing the usage data. The five months of evidence we have says people pay for
**grammar**, which supports the owner's course instinct more than my
recommendation. Mock exams and writing had only two days of exposure, so it is
not settled — but do not repeat the exam-pack recommendation as if it were
evidence-backed.

**Realistic timeline, told to the owner plainly:** €10,000/month is **18–30
months** away, not three. Ankommo, with 107k followers, is probably making low
four figures — so €10k is not "catch up to him", it is "be several times bigger".

## 9. Environment gotchas that cost time

- **Egress proxy blocks most domains** — including `deutsch-meister.de`,
  `*.netlify.app`, `supabase.co`, and all competitor sites. Workarounds:
  `mcp__Composio__COMPOSIO_REMOTE_BASH_TOOL` (unrestricted curl), Firecrawl
  search, and the MCP connectors (Supabase/Netlify/GitHub all work fine).
- **Playwright** must be reinstalled per session (`npm i --no-save playwright`);
  browsers live at `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`. Scratchpad scripts must resolve it
  via `createRequire('/home/user/deutschmeister/package.json')`.
- **The Astro build outputs to `astro-site/dist`, not repo-root `dist`.** I lost
  time serving a stale repo-root `dist`. The repo-root `dist` is the *merged* SPA
  + Astro output produced by the full CI-mirror sequence.
- `cd` persists between Bash calls — it silently broke a relative path mid-session.
- **`astro-site/package-lock.json` churn**: the session-start hook rewrites it.
  Convention is `git checkout --` to restore, **never commit it**.
- `scripts/prerender-spa-routes.mjs` is **not idempotent** — it strips the
  canonical from `app.html`; rebuild from scratch before re-running.
- DataForSEO works via the **direct API** (`$DATAFORSEO_USERNAME` /
  `$DATAFORSEO_PASSWORD`) even when its MCP is down.
