DeutschMeister competitor study — supplied capture set

The strongest direction is to let visitors experience one useful German exercise before asking them to evaluate Pro. Use Duolingo for action hierarchy, Speak for explaining the speaking loop, Praktika for showing a correction, and Busuu for placing evidence beside the start button. Babbel’s pricing page is the clearest reference for purchase terms. Rebuild these patterns with our own material through REA.

Preview v3 already has a distinct identity and useful reassurance. Its weakness is that the trial button appears before visitors can inspect a complete exercise. Live offers a concrete start, but English copy, competing choices, and cookies add friction.

Preview home’s “beste Adresse” is an aspiration, not evidence. Its telc page earns trust by saying booking is unavailable, but the proposed 20-unit course is not proof of delivered inventory. Live describes level purchases; preview promotes Pro. Resolve the actual offer before any experiment, and verify trial terms rather than treating screenshot copy as a product audit.

**Three experiments.** Run separately against unchanged v3. Use consented measurement; report denominators. Each trial ends after 14 days or 100 eligible sessions per variant, whichever comes first. Small samples are directional.

1. **Onboarding: show the first task.** Replace the home headline with “Deutsch üben. Mit einer ersten Aufgabe.” Remove the aspiration paragraph and move the existing A1.1 exercise above the fold. Use “Beginnen Sie mit einer Aufgabe aus A1.1.” Primary button: “Erste Aufgabe starten”; secondary: “Passendes Niveau finden”. Route to the actual exercise; verify access requirements before launch. Mechanism: reduce commitment and uncertainty. Measure first-task completion per landing session, median time to first answer, and abandonment at any account gate. Stop immediately for misleading access promises; otherwise retain only if completion improves without worsening abandonment. Inconclusive results keep the control.

2. **Speaking loop: demonstrate a retry.** Add a compact working card: “Sprechen Sie. Prüfen Sie die Rückmeldung. Versuchen Sie es erneut.” Prompt: “Stellen Sie sich kurz vor: Wie heißen Sie, und wo wohnen Sie?” Buttons: “Aufnahme starten”, “Rückmeldung ansehen”, “Noch einmal sprechen”; fallback: “Antwort tippen”. Use actual returned feedback, identified as “KI-Rückmeldung”. Launch only after this loop works; otherwise label a recorded team demonstration “Aufgezeichnetes Beispiel”. Mechanism: make feedback useful through immediate repetition. Measure successful feedback delivery and second recordings per first recording, plus response time and failures. Stop for a recording/privacy defect or failed-feedback rate above 5%; at the endpoint, keep only if retries improve without more failures.

3. **Proof: replace assertion with inspectable work.** Replace the home aspiration with “Sehen Sie eine Aufgabe und ihre Rückmeldung.” Add “Beispiel ansehen” beside the main CTA, opening an actual team-tested exercise and its captured correction, labeled “Beispiel aus einem internen Test”. Preserve telc honesty: “Der Kurs ist derzeit nicht buchbar. Sehen Sie sich einen Musterbrief an.” Link only to an available example. Mechanism: let visitors judge the work. Measure example views, subsequent exercise completions, and whether five usability participants understand what is available. Stop if any example is unreproducible or mistaken for a learner testimonial; retain only if completion improves and availability remains clear.

**Social proof we can earn in 30 days.**

- Days 1–7: publish a dated changelog and a clear available/planned feature list. These demonstrate delivery, not learning outcomes.
- Days 1–14: invite initial adult testers through an opt-in route. Request candid feedback after a completed task; never condition access or rewards on praise.
- Days 8–21: obtain explicit publication permission for exact quotes, attribution, channels, and duration. Offer pseudonyms and withdrawal; disclose incentives. Do not publish private corrections or recordings without separate permission.
- Days 8–21: publish a truthful team line naming actual responsibilities and verified qualifications, with permission. No invented teacher status or affiliations.
- Days 15–30: count distinct, published, usable exam-practice tasks from the database, excluding drafts and duplicates. Publish the date and definition; do not imply official exam approval.
- By day 30: show a real exercise, tested correction, and honest availability even without reviews. Report small pilot results with their sample and method; do not turn completion into a claim of exam success.
