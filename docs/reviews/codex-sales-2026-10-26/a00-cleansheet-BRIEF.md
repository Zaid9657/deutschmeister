# Clean-sheet design — blind

You are designing a business FROM ZERO. You have NOT seen the founder's existing product, website, code or plans, and you must not ask for them. That is deliberate: we want the answer you would give with no anchor.

## The goal (fixed — never changes)
DeutschMeister reaches $1M a year in revenue (≈ €73k a month, VAT-exclusive, after refunds), run mostly by AI agents with limited founder time. Everything else — product, offer, price, audience, website, channels, plan — is yours to choose.

## The situation (all you get)
- Founder: a physician working in Germany (Blue Card), solo, limited time and energy after clinical work. Builds with AI agents (Claude, Codex) that can write code, pages and copy; the founder must personally approve money, legal, brand messages and outward posting. Has a working web stack (static site + web app + payments via Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record + Supabase + serverless functions), a domain deutsch-meister.de, and ~1,750 free accounts collected so far, ~15 new signups a week, almost no revenue (~€270 lifetime).
- Founder's own north-star elsewhere: build an engaged Telegram audience that converts to paying customers.
- Demand evidence (from the free signups, last 60 days, which exam they named): Goethe B1 45, Goethe A1 11, telc B2 11, Goethe A2 10, telc B1 10, DTZ 8; 9 "none", 129 didn't say. Only 4 of 1,750 gave an exam date. Signups mostly come from Google; brand searches dominate.
- Market facts (2025): ~318,550 DTZ (Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer) takers, ~332,500 naturalisations (B1 required), ~461,000 Goethe B1 exams worldwide. German exam-name searches ≈34,000/month (telc B1/B2 and DTZ ≈95%). Migration pipelines abroad (India, Indonesia, nursing) need Goethe/telc B1/B2.
- Competitors and prices: see ./market/ (live search results and offer pages captured today) if present.
- Hard constraints: German consumer law (Widerruf, Impressum, PAngV), possible FernUSG (distance-learning) approval if live instruction/assignments are monitored, UWG on advertising claims, GDPR; no fake reviews; no outcome guarantees ("you will pass"); the founder cannot run live classes.

## Deliver (Markdown, 1,500–2,500 words)
1. The business you would build: who buys first, what exactly they buy, at what price, why they buy it from you instead of the alternatives in ./market/. Give 3 genuinely different models (e.g. exam-sprint product, AI tutor subscription, B2B/teacher tool, community-led, marketplace, done-for-you correction service, app-store app…), score each on revenue potential in 12 months, time-to-first-€, founder hours, legal risk, AI-cost risk. Pick one.
2. The website/funnel you would build for it from scratch: page list, the one-sentence hero, the first-screen offer, the free hook, the checkout moment, the language(s).
3. Distribution: the first 1,000 buyers' path — channels in order, with weekly founder minutes.
4. Pricing and offer ladder for the first 90 days.
5. The 30-day launch plan, week by week, split founder vs AI agents.
6. What would prove you wrong in 30 days (kill rules).
