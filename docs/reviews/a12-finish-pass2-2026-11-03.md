## Reading review and test migration

The old 46/64-answer and 10/16-whole-paper feature-pair results described a **binary answer surface**. That surface has been replaced, not shuffled. Its original bank is preserved in `tests/fixtures/a12-lesen-legacy.json`; the old semantic annotations, feature strategies, equality ratchets and premature-feedback mutation still run on that historical fixture (`tests/a12-rebuild.test.mjs:16`, `tests/checkpoint-feedback.test.mjs:61`). Current-paper tests exercise the new typed surface separately. An A/B strategy now has no buttons to select; reporting this as an apples-to-apples “46/64 → 0/64” improvement would be misleading. No claim of immunity to every guessing strategy is made.

I read all 64 displayed texts against their new frames/keys, and checked the final test's eight statements/keys separately. The new extraction frames and the 128 source-authored assessment tasks were reviewed by their author in this run, **not by a separate teacher or reviewer**. The 64 underlying texts retain their earlier independent review. The tests check many structural and language properties, but cannot establish general semantic correctness. U01's mirrored-choice mechanism is removed; its independent re-review requirement remains open.

Other changed expectations are explicit: the practice-exposure ratchet is lowered to zero; the tense inventory becomes 414 servable items; German task formulas inside parentheses use the existing shared formula list. A bracket tokenizer was corrected so `Uhr]` is recognized as a temporal cue. Existing assertions about unknown German cue words, tense selectors, accepted alternatives, A1.1 behavior, scoring, purchases and saved progress remain enabled. No failing suite is skipped.
