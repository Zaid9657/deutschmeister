## 1. The one-paragraph thesis

The A1 buyer is not "someone learning German"; it is someone who must score **60/100 on
Start Deutsch 1 by a fixed date** — above all the ~36–42k spouse-visa candidates a year
(Bundestag Drs. 21/175), 90% preparing without a course, 35–38% failing, pass rates under
40% in Turkey, the largest sending country. Free DW Nicos Weg owns the "learn German" half
and is unanimously faulted for the other half: no speaking, no writing, no exam tasks, no
review. Goethe grades by e-mail for €499+; Udemy sells video with no feedback; the AI mock
apps (€7–70 passes) grade but teach nothing. **The empty slot is a course that teaches the
A1 curriculum AND grades every spoken and written exam task instantly, planned backward
from the buyer's exam date, at a one-time price with no expiry.** That is exactly the AI
stack we already run — and nothing else in the market combines the two.

## 2. The product definition (what "best" means, concretely)

The course is **"Start Deutsch 1 — bestanden"**, not "German A1". Its spine:

1. **Day 1:** ask exam date and purpose; 25-minute diagnostic in the four real task
   types scored on the 60/100 rule; a screen of exam facts (no per-section minimum,
   retakes unlimited, fee €80–230 by country); time-of-day commitment prompt (the one
   planning tactic with completion evidence — Cho 2024).
2. **A dated backward plan** (8–12 weeks standard; last 3–4 weeks exam-format), with a
   stated hour count (~60–80 h floor; tell non-Latin-script learners to budget double).
3. **The four SD1 task types drilled to automation**, point schemes visible:
   Hören (3 parts, 15 items, Teil 2 heard once, native tempo) · Lesen (notes, Anzeigen,
   Schilder) · Schreiben (5-field Formular + 30-word Mitteilung with Anrede/Gruß) ·
   Sprechen Teil 1–3 (sich vorstellen + Buchstabieren; Informationen erfragen with word
   cards; **Bitten formulieren** with picture cards).
4. **AI speaking on every Sprechen card** graded on the 3/6/6 scheme; **AI writing on
   every Formular/Mitteilung** graded on the named Goethe criteria. This is the moat: no
   competitor at any price combines curriculum + instant grading.
5. **The official ~650-word A1 Wortliste as a spaced-review deck**, tied to lessons —
   answers the universal "nothing comes back" complaint (spacing: Kim & Webb 2022).
6. **≥5 full timed mocks** per learner (the 3 official free sets + our own variants),
   per-skill trend against the pass line; "ready when your last two mocks score ≥70".
7. **Post-fail path**: a 4-week re-plan targeting the weakest two sections (35–38% of
   spouse candidates fail; nobody serves them).
8. Mobile-first, low-bandwidth (buyers are in Turkey, India, Kosovo, Pakistan).

## 3. The honest gap (our A1 today vs that definition)

The content audit's verdict, unsoftened: today's A1 is **a competent ~5-hour grammar
reference with good listening dialogues and well-built speaking missions** — not a course.
Sold as "the best A1 course" it would not survive contact with an SD1 candidate.

| Area | State | Worst finding |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar topics (16) | half good, half thin | `present-tense-regular` omits stem changes → teaches "du arbeitst"; job-article contradiction; possessives/separable verbs/Ja-Nein-Fragen/imperative/Uhrzeit missing from A1 entirely |
| Exercises (176) | all recognition | every "fill-blank" ships options; ~30 are English meta-quizzes; zero typed production |
| Reading (16) | mixed | half the texts are A2 (Perfekt, weil-clauses); Goethe A1 texts are 50–100 words, ours up to 289; no Anzeigen/Schilder |
| Listening (12) | good | thin questioning (1 per dialogue), no number dictation |
| Vocabulary (472) | thin | ~250 Wortliste words missing (no Länder, Berufe, Getränke, Uhrzeit); 12 plurals are the string "null"; **0 words have audio** while the page advertises audio |
| Speaking (16 missions) | good | Sprechen Teil 3 (Bitten) has no mission; imperative untaught |
| Writing | **absent** | zero A1 tasks (all four existing tasks are B1/B2) |
| A1 mock exam | **absent** | mocks exist only for B1/B2 exams |
| Plan / final test / audio | absent | no day plan, no completion test, no audio anywhere outside 12 listening files |

Total estimated content work to the full definition: **~200 hours**. Keep as-is: listening,
speaking missions, accusative, negation, articles, sein/haben.

## 4. Price and law (from the pricing track)

- **€49 for A1 is credible ONLY with the SD1 mock + writing strand shipped.** Against free
  Nicos Weg, €9.99 Ankommo and $14 Udemy, €49-without-a-mock is an app price without an
  app. The pricing report's call: launch at €49 only when the mock lands; A2/B1/B2 hold
  €49; consider a €79 A2+B1 pair as the featured card later (B1 buyers buy A2+B1).
- **FernUSG (BGH III ZR 109/24, III ZR 173/24, 2025):** a paid, mostly-async course with a
  contractual right to individual feedback is Fernunterricht → needs ZFU approval or the
  contract is void. Therefore: never promise personal feedback in the course; the AI
  grading is an **automated tool**, worded as such, delivered via Pro.
- **Never claim:** guaranteed pass, "A1 in X weeks", that our certificate counts for any
  visa (only Goethe/telc/ÖSD/DTZ do), un-earned strike prices (§11 PAngV), or marketing
  the statutory Widerruf as a "guarantee" (BGH I ZR 185/12). A voluntary 30-day refund is
  a real value-add and standard at this price (Anja, Udemy, Babbel).

## 5. Build sequence (proposed — nothing started)

Ordered by "what makes €49 honest" per hour of work. Agent-buildable except where noted.

| # | Work | Effort | Why first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fix wrong/contradictory grammar** (stem-change note, job-article contradiction, es-pronoun garble, out-of-level exercise items) | ~8 h | We are selling access to it today |
| 2 | **A1 writing strand**: 6 Formular + 6 Mitteilung tasks on the existing AI-writing runner, Goethe criteria in the rubric | ~12 h | Half the moat; nothing exists |
| 3 | **A1 mock exam** (Goethe SD1 format) on the existing mock runner + the 3 official sets linked | ~20 h | The line that justifies €49 |
| 4 | **Day-by-day plan** ("30 Tage bis Start Deutsch 1") on the telc-B1-plan rails, with exam-date input | ~10 h | The dated-plan spine |
| 5 | **Sprechen Teil 1–3 missions** (full self-intro, word cards, Bitten/imperative) | ~6 h | Fear #1 for buyers |
| 6 | **Missing grammar topics** (possessives, separable verbs, Ja/Nein-Fragen, imperative, Uhrzeit/numbers upgrade) | ~30 h | Exam coverage |
| 7 | **Wortliste completion + SRS wiring** (+250 words, fix "null" plurals, feed the existing SRS) | ~25 h | The review loop |
| 8 | **Audio** via the existing `generate-example-audio.mjs` + TTS (owner: OpenAI key run) | script exists | Removes a false claim on the vocab page |
| 9 | Reading rewrites (7 texts ≤120 words, Anzeigen/Schilder), listening question depth, typed-production exercises | ~60 h | Depth, after the spine |

Steps 1–5 (~56 h of content work, agent-executable in sessions) make the €49 A1 course
honest and differentiated. Steps 6–9 make it complete.
