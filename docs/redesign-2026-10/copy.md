# Copy deck: "Die Linie"

**The deck in code:**
- Hero variants live in `astro-site/src/data/homepage.js`.
- Offer copy lives in `src/data/offers.js`, a twin of the Astro copy guarded by `check-duplicates`.
- Figures are never typed. They are filled from `pricing.js`, `marketing.js` and `courseContents.js`, and `tests/claims.test.mjs` and `tests/offers.test.mjs` fail on a typed price.

## Voice

- **Plain English.** German appears only where it is the content: *Guten Tag*, *Im Bau*, *Einzelfahrschein*, *Lektion*.
- **Concrete over clever.** "One payment. No subscription." beats any line about freedom.
- **The course speaks *Sie*.** Marketing English has no register problem, but every German string on a commerce surface stays formal (CLAUDE.md, the A1.1 rule).
- **Never claimed:**
  - learner counts;
  - testimonials;
  - "most popular";
  - scarcity or deadlines;
  - pass rates or "fluent in N";
  - refund promises (an owner decision; see `strategy.md` §7).

## The ticket (what one payment buys)

These four lines are generated by `ticketLines(level)`. Each also completes a "No more …" line.

1. **{code}, yours to keep.** The guided plan (days, steps, hours), the reading lessons, the listening sets and questions, the vocabulary and the speaking missions, ending in a final test in exam format. *No more guessing what to study next.*
2. **3 months of Pro on board.** Every level opens, with 30 AI speaking sessions and 20 writing corrections a month and 50 X-Ray analyses a day. *No more practising speaking and writing with no one to correct you.*
3. **One payment. No subscription.** Nothing renews. When the 3 months end, the level stays and the AI returns to the free allowance. *No more paying every month for a level you finish once.*
4. **Your progress, saved.** Every answer is saved to your account, on any device. *No more starting over on a new device.*
