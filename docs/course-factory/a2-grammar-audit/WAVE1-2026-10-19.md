## Conventions used in the SQL

- **Audit markup dropped.** The audit is Markdown: its fixes write *glauben*, *nach* in asterisks and wrap each fix in
  „…" or "…". The grammar content holds no Markdown at all (0 asterisks in 933 examples and 672 rules), and
  `RichText.astro` prints asterisks literally, so the italics markers and the outer quotation marks are not copied.
  Every other character of a fix is copied exactly.
- **Splice.** Where the audit quotes one sentence of a longer field, only that sentence is replaced and the rest of
  the field is kept (`splice`). Where the fix restates the whole field, the whole field is replaced.
- **twin.** The audit's PARITY rule (Part 1, §4) wants both language fields to say the same thing. Where the audit
  names one field and its `_en`/`_de` twin in the same rule or example states the same false claim, the twin is fixed
  too. These statements are marked `twin`; dropping them leaves the twin wrong on the public page (it renders the
  English).
- **authored.** The audit names the field and the problem but gives no text, or says "the English twin is to
  match". The text is written here in the house style (Sie, exact German, English twin). Needs review.
- **≠cache.** The audit's "Now" quote is not the exact cache text (abbreviated, or `…` for `...`). The guard uses the
  cache text.
- One statement per field. Where two items touch one field, one statement carries both fixes and both rows point to it.
