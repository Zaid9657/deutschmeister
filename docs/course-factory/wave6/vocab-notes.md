# Wave 6 PR A — Wortliste re-level notes

Source: `S/vocab/source/words-a2.2-prep-animals.json` (50 rows: 25 Animals, 25 Prepositions).
Output: `S/vocab/relevel.json`. No example sentences were edited (per brief, Wave 5 already
rewrote the preposition sentences to avoid Genitiv/banned forms). No row was moved below a2.2.

## Method

Judged each row against the Goethe-Zertifikat A2 Wortliste (Erwachsene) from memory, using the
brief's own anchors (Hund/Katze/Pferd/Kuh/Schwein/Vogel/Fisch/Maus/Tier and außer/gegenüber/
seit/bis(zu) confirmed A2; trotz/wegen/während/statt/anstatt confirmed B1; the -halb/-seits/
inmitten/infolge/aufgrund/anhand/mittels/mithilfe/zufolge group confirmed B1–B2 with mittels/
zufolge/diesseits/jenseits/unweit/inmitten named as clearly B2). Where the brief gave no anchor
(entlang, laut's exact tier, and every animal), I made the call from general knowledge of Goethe
word-list scope and everyday frequency, documented below.

## Animals (25 rows) — none of these are core A1/A2 animal words

The A2 Wortliste's animal coverage is exhausted by the brief's own anchor list (Hund, Katze,
Pferd, Kuh, Schwein, Vogel, Fisch, Maus, Tier) — none of which appear in this batch, confirming
the brief's "mostly not on the A2 list" framing extends to all 25 here. I split the movers by
everyday-encounter frequency in German life rather than by grammar topic (there is no
animal-vocabulary grammar topic to anchor to, unlike the prepositions):

- **b1.1 (17 words)** — animals a learner plausibly meets outside a zoo (garden/city/farm/
  forest/household): Eichhörnchen, Igel, Fuchs, Wolf, Bär, Eule, Frosch, Schlange, Spinne, Biene,
  Ameise, Schmetterling, Mücke, Gans, Schaf, Ziege, Kaninchen. Several of these (Wolf, Biene) are
  also topical in German public discourse (wolves returning to Germany, Bienensterben/insect
  protection), which raises their exposure at B1 reading level.
- **b1.2 (8 words)** — zoo/aquarium/exotic animals with lower everyday frequency: Delfin, Hai,
  Wal, Pinguin, Papagei, Adler, Hirsch, Schildkröte.

**Doubts for the reviewer**: Mücke (mosquito) is a very common summer-conversation word and I
went back and forth on a2.2 vs b1.1 — I judged it above the Goethe A2 Wortliste and kept it at
b1.1, but a reviewer with more direct Wortliste access may downgrade it. Eichhörnchen (squirrel)
is also unusually frequent in everyday urban German (city parks) for a "b1.1" word; I kept it
there because it is still not on the core A1/A2 list. None of the 25 struck me as clearly B2 —
I did not use b2.1 for any animal.

## Prepositions (25 rows)

**Stayed at a2.2 (5)** — words the brief itself anchors as A2, or that are already established
A1/A2.1 grammar:
- außer, bis zu, gegenüber, seit — directly named as A2 in the brief (gegenüber and seit are
  also already-productive A1/A2.1 dative prepositions per `level-a2.2.md`).
- **entlang** — the brief flagged this explicitly as "(A2? — decide and justify)". I judged it
  A2: it is Wegbeschreibung vocabulary ("die Straße entlang gehen"), a staple of A1/A2
  direction-giving units, and I believe it is on the Goethe A2 Wortliste. This is my lowest-
  confidence call in the whole set — flagging for reviewer double-check.

**b1.1 (11)** — the genitive-case topic's core, everyday-register words: anstatt, aufgrund,
außerhalb, innerhalb, laut, oberhalb, statt, trotz, unterhalb, während, wegen. trotz/wegen/
während/statt/anstatt are the brief's own "B1" anchors; laut is the brief's "(B1)"-flagged item;
aufgrund/außerhalb/innerhalb/oberhalb/unterhalb are common genitive prepositions I judged
frequent enough to teach alongside the b1.1 genitive-case topic itself.

**b1.2 (3)** — same grammar topic (genitive-case, taught at b1.1) but formal/written register I
judged less urgent for early-B1 vocabulary pacing: anhand, infolge, mithilfe. These sit inside
the brief's "-halb/-seits/inmitten/infolge/aufgrund/anhand/mittels/mithilfe/zufolge is B1–B2"
group; I split aufgrund off to b1.1 (very frequent even in speech) and kept anhand/infolge/
mithilfe at b1.2 (more written/explanatory register: anhand von Beispielen, infolge von, mithilfe
von).

**b2.1 (6)** — exactly the brief's named "clearly B2" list: diesseits, inmitten, jenseits,
mittels, unweit, zufolge. All six are literary, bureaucratic, or news-register words rare in
everyday spoken German; I did not second-guess this explicit anchor.

## Open doubts for the reviewer

1. **entlang** at a2.2 — lowest-confidence single call in this batch (see above).
2. **Mücke** and **Eichhörnchen** at b1.1 vs a2.2 — both are higher-frequency in daily speech
   than most of the b1.1 group; kept above the floor but worth a second opinion.
3. The b1.1/b1.2 animal split has no grammar-topic anchor (unlike prepositions, which map to the
   genitive-case topic) — it is a frequency judgment call, not a Wortliste-tier fact, and a
   reviewer may want to collapse it to a single b1.1 bucket or re-split differently.
4. **anhand vs aufgrund** — both are in the brief's same B1–B2 group; I judged aufgrund
   meaningfully more frequent (spoken + written) and anhand more written-only, but the gap
   between them is a judgment call, not a hard fact.
