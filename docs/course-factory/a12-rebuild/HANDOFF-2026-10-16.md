
- **Grammar boxes (notices).**
  - At most 60 words, with exactly two examples quoted verbatim from the dialogue.
  - Every bold form needs a dialogue line that uses it (RULE 6b) and must appear in the English
    twin.
  - Bold whole phrases („gefällt mir", „vor einer"), never a bare common word: a bold „vor" or
    „mir" marks every earlier card that uses the word as teaching a later form.
- **Dialogues.** 6–10 lines, at most 12 words each, two speakers. Every question is annotated in the
  M6 test.
- **Wortfeld.** Every word needs an input line (RULE 23). An irregular form the dialogue uses goes
  into `IRREGULAR_FORMS_A12`; the A1.1 table stays as it is, so its measured numbers don't move.
