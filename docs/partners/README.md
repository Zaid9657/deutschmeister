# Partner kit — owner drafts

This kit supports move 4, “Line up the buyers before opening”, in the
[strategy](../strategy/million-plan-2026-10.md). It prepares conversations with
telc teachers, course providers and community admins before the telc B1 pilot
opens. The offer is not on sale yet. Keep `[Startdatum]` and `[Preis]` as
placeholders until the owner confirms them.

**Everything here is a draft for the owner. Agents never send or post any of
it.** Only the owner chooses recipients, sends messages, handles replies and
authorizes any community placement. Preparing this kit does not open sales.

## How to use it

1. Review the [German offer](partner-offer.de.md). Its product facts come only
   from [offers.js](../../src/data/offers.js),
   [pricing.js](../../src/data/pricing.js) and
   [marketing.js](../../src/data/marketing.js). Recheck included Pro access and
   AI limits before sharing; these are current source facts, not final launch
   terms. No course-access duration beyond the included Pro window is stated.
2. Pick an [outreach template](outreach-templates.de.md), replace the recipient,
   personal line and sender placeholders, and review every claim against the
   offer. Leave the launch date and price placeholders intact while unconfirmed.
3. Assign a placeholder slug using the [link scheme](links.md). Check the
   destination and tags locally before the owner uses a link. The examples are
   information-page links, not checkout or reservation links.
4. Record the recipient, slug, template, owner send status, reply, next step and
   owner time in a private owner log. Interest is not a purchase or a reservation.

No sample-feedback demo is included: that requires a real AI grading run. Do
not invent feedback, grades, learner results, endorsements or partner terms.
This kit offers no commission or payout agreement.

## Weekly batch: 10 messages a week

- **Monday:** Prepare 10 personalized drafts for owner review, starting with
  telc teachers and course providers. Follow-ups, if any, count toward the same
  weekly total of 10 messages.
- **One owner batch:** The owner reviews and sends the 10 messages, subject to
  the strategy's release gate and documented outreach basis. Hold any message
  that is not ready; agents do not send it to meet the weekly target.
- **Friday:** Review replies and owner time, record objections and interest,
  and choose the next week's recipients. The first 1–3 weekly batches cover the
  strategy's initial 10–30 approaches.

For a community test, the owner may arrange the strategy's single tightly
capped, tagged placement with the admin. Agree its scope and cap before posting;
agents only draft it. Judge it by qualified buyers, not reactions or membership.

The owner opens the sale only after the strategy's release gate passes. Track
qualified offer views separately from replies and eventual paid sales. Apply
move 4's decision rule only after at least 300 qualified offer views; fewer than
3 sales at that exposure calls for one package change, not a price change.
The current link parser alone does not supply that view denominator or reliable
individual-partner attribution; see the limitations in [links.md](links.md).

**Open before first use (owner decision):** the speaking allowance. The site says 30 AI speaking
sessions a month; the server gives Pro two 5-minute sessions a day (no monthly quota). The offer
sheet leaves it as a placeholder until you decide whether the copy or the server changes.
