## 2. Synthesis

### (a) Anatomy of a professional A1 "Lektion" (what teachers expect)

Every mainstream Lehrwerk and the two serious online providers (Lingoda, Goethe DO) share one skeleton; only the granularity differs (Menschen = 4 pages/4 UE; Schritte = 14 UE; Lingoda = 4 × 60 min).

1. **Lernziele up front** – 3–5 can-do lines in the learner's words ("Ich kann nach dem Weg fragen"), visible before the content (Menschen Kasten, Nicos Weg header, Lingoda "I can…").
2. **Einstieg** – a situation (photo story / video / dialogue) that *contains* the target language before it is named.
3. **Wortfeld** – 15–25 words as a picture lexicon, tied to one Handlungsfeld (BAMF list above).
4. **2–3 Lernschritte**, each = one structure or one Sprachhandlung + Redemittel box, presented in a text then drilled (Schritte A–C).
5. **Skills block** – one Hören *and* one Lesen text, then one Schreiben (Formular/SMS/E-Mail/Postkarte) and one Sprechen task (Dialogkarte / Bitte formulieren) — i.e. the four Goethe parts in miniature.
6. **Phonetik** micro-slot (one sound/intonation pattern).
7. **Grammatik + Kommunikation overview** – one page of tables and Redemittel.
8. **Test + Selbstevaluation** ("Das kann ich" checklist) and a **Lernwortschatz** list.
9. Every 3–4 Lektionen: **Plateau / Modul-Plus / Haltestelle** = Wiederholung, Landeskunde, project, exam-format training.

### (b) Standard topic sequence and whether "12 topics per half-level" matches

The field's half-level sizes: Menschen 12, Lingoda 12 chapters, Netzwerk 6, Linie 1 8, Schritte/Pluspunkt/DUO 7, Goethe DO 9 (18/level). **12 per half-level is on the fine-grained end but exactly matches Menschen and Lingoda — the two most-copied designs — so it is defensible**; what the field adds is a review checkpoint every 3 units (4 per half-level). ### (d) A curriculum page that beats the field

Nobody shows all of this before purchase; the best partial examples are Goethe DO's 18-row grid, Lingoda's chapter cards + sample PDFs, and DUO's per-page Lernziel + minutes. Beat them with one public page per half-level:

1. **Grid, 12 rows × 6 columns**: Nr · Titel/Situation · Kann-Beschreibungen (Goethe wording) · Wortfeld (count, e.g. "+28 → 312/650") · Grammatik · Goethe-Teil trained (e.g. "Sprechen T3 Bitten"). Downloadable as PDF like Goethe's.
2. **Unit anatomy diagram** showing the 9-step Lektion skeleton with minutes per step and the total ("≈4 h 20 min ≈ 5–6 UE").
3. **Checkpoint markers** after units 3/6/9/12 (Wiederholung + mini-Modelltest in official format), and a **Level test = full Modellsatz** with the 60-point pass rule — state explicitly "no exam certificate; prepares for Goethe-Zertifikat A1".
4. **Running counters**: words introduced vs Wortliste, hours vs GI guidance (A1 80–200 UE), can-dos covered vs the Prüfungsziele list, exam Teile rehearsed (11 at A1, 13 at A2).
5. **One sample unit fully open** (Seedlang/Lingoda pattern) plus its test.
6. **Provenance line**: "Kann-Beschreibungen: Goethe Prüfungsziele SD1 (2016); Wortliste: Goethe A2 (2016); Themen: BAMF Rahmencurriculum (2022 rev.)" — the thing every app omits and every teacher looks for.
