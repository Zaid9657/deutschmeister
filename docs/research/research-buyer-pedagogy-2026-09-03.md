# Buyer & pedagogy research — level courses (A1 first; B1/DTZ retained)

Researched 2026-09-03 via Perplexity search + Firecrawl (Perplexity deep-research was down; vendor sites not fetched directly). "Not found" = no published figure located.

## PART A1 — THE A1 BUYER (primary)

### Who takes Goethe/telc A1 and why
- **Ehegattennachzug is the engine.** §30 AufenthG requires "einfache Deutschkenntnisse" = A1 before the visa; Goethe's Start Deutsch 1 is the accepted proof (goethe.de/de/spr/prf). Bundestag Drs. 21/175 (May 2025): **41,869 SD1 exams "im Rahmen des Ehegattennachzugs" in 2023, 27,294 passed (65%); 35,720 in 2024, 22,278 passed (62%)**. ~90% are external candidates (no Goethe course); only ~20% attend a Goethe course, and a quarter of those still fail. **>10,000 spouses/year are refused a visa (at first) for failing** (13,607 in 2022). Country pass rates <50% in 2022: North Macedonia, Nigeria, Sudan, Bangladesh, Kenya, Sri Lanka, Mexico; **<40% in Cameroon and Turkey**. 72,552 spouse visas issued 2024 (78,644 first residence permits); top origins Turkey (8,384), India (6,773), Kosovo (4,968), Russia, Iran, Albania, Bosnia, Pakistan, Serbia, North Macedonia (Drs. 21/175; welt.de 2025-05-22). Spouses of Germans need A1 too; EU-citizen spouses, Blue-Card/highly-skilled spouses, US/Israel etc. are exempt (zeit.de 2019).
- Others: first-year arrivals (self-payers who cannot get an Integrationskurs slot after the **Feb 2026 BAMF Zulassungsstopp**, see B1 part), au-pairs (A1 recommended for the visa), Chancenkarte points (A1 German), students' partners. Counts: not found.
- Germany-wide A1 exam totals (Goethe + telc): **not published**.

### Format (Goethe SD1; telc Start Deutsch 1 is near-identical)
Written 65 min, no breaks: **Hören ~20 min** (3 parts, 15 items; Teil 1 six dialogues ×2, Teil 2 four announcements ×1, Teil 3 five phone messages ×2) — **Lesen 25 min** (3 parts, 15 items: R/F on short letters, choose the matching ad, signs/notices) — **Schreiben 20 min** (Teil 1: fill 5 fields of a form, 5 pts; Teil 2: ~30-word note/e-mail on 3 Leitpunkte with Anrede/Gruß, 10 pts). **Sprechen 15 min**, group of up to 4, no prep time: Teil 1 sich vorstellen + buchstabieren + Nummer (3 pts, ~80 s), Teil 2 Informationen erfragen/geben with word cards (6 pts), Teil 3 Bitten formulieren/darauf reagieren with picture cards (6 pts). Raw 60 pts scaled to 100 (25/25/25/25); **pass = 60/100 overall, no per-section minimum**; <35/75 written makes the oral pointless (Goethe Durchführungsbestimmungen; Prüfungsziele PDF). Retakes unlimited.

### Which section fails
Not published officially. Reddit self-reports (2024–26): failed-candidate breakdown "Hören 14.9, Lesen 10, Schreiben 11.6, Sprechen 10 = 46/100"; passers report **Hören** as the hardest (announcements once only, all three options appear in the audio), **Lesen** "toughest" for some, **Sprechen** most *feared* but "graded leniently, they want you to pass", **Schreiben** the section most people never practise ("I struggled immensely with Schreiben"). Philippines forum reports a Goethe sitting where ~10 of 100 passed (2019). Consistent picture: fear = Sprechen; actual point loss = Hören/Lesen + unprepared Schreiben.

### Forum pain (r/German, r/Germanlearning, spouse-visa threads)
1. **Deadline panic**: "I've got a month to get to A1, how f*cked am I" — visa appointment fixed, exam slots scarce.
2. **No speaking partner** ("hardest was speaking, I had no one to study with").
3. Didn't know the **official A1 word list** existed until a week before.
4. Studying "German" instead of the exam: "you don't need to speak German, you need to repeat the tasks effortlessly"; passers memorise sentence frames (Ich heiße/komme aus/möchte…, Können Sie mir bitte helfen?).
5. Listening speed; Lieber/Liebe and verb-position errors in the note; nerves in the group oral.
6. Failing after "3 h/day for 2 months" of unstructured apps — apps alone do not produce the form/e-mail/Bitte tasks.
Wish-list: mock exams, a per-section score, a fixed daily routine (one blog's Mon–Sun plan is the format people ask for), someone to correct the 30-word text.

### What A1 prep costs today
- **Goethe exam fee**: Germany €155 (€124 for course participants) (goethe.de Preise 2026); India ₹7,500–9,400 (~€80–100); Morocco 1,700–1,950 DH (~€160–180). Bundestag: average Goethe course + exam in main sending countries **€494** (Drs. 21/56).
- **Courses**: Goethe India A1 ₹22–28k (~€240–300); Indian third-party institutes ₹8–18k; Morocco private schools 1,500–4,500 DH/level, tutors 100–300 DH/h; Goethe Brazil A1.1 8-week 4,062 BRL (~€650). Germany: VHS/Integrationskurs €229 per 100-UE module with BAMF slot, **€458–470 self-pay**, private intensive €3,000–5,000 to B1. Tutors: Preply German $18–28/h avg, italki $5–35/h.
- Anchor: €49 is <1/3 of one exam fee and <1/10 of the €494 Goethe path abroad; in the sending countries it competes with ₹1,500–4,000 prep platforms and free DW "Nicos Weg".

### Pedagogy for absolute beginners
- **Hours**: Goethe FAQ 60–150 guided hours to A1 (b1goethe.com citing Goethe Belgium); Cambridge 90–100 GLH for same-script learners, 180–240 for different-script; practitioners report 80–120 total hours, i.e. **2–4 months at 5 h/week, 4–6 weeks intensive** (germanlesson.online). A Reddit passer did it in one month at ~1 h/day by drilling the exam only. Plan for 60–80 h minimum, tell non-Latin-script buyers (Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Cyrillic) to budget double.
- **Alphabet/script**: teach Buchstabieren + numbers + spelling names/addresses in week 1 — it is literally Sprechen Teil 1 and the form task; Latin-script learners need 1–2 sessions, others ongoing dictation drills.
- **First-week wins**: self-introduction (name/age/country/languages/job/hobby) recorded and scored by day 3; a filled Anmeldeformular; ten question-word frames. These are exam points, not warm-ups.
- **Output realism**: formulaic chunks first (usage-based SLA; "sentence frames" is what every passer describes), 30-word notes, ~1-minute monologues. Evidence base: spacing (Kim & Webb 2022 medium-to-large), retrieval with feedback (MALL meta g≈0.9), explicit corrective feedback (Li 2010 d=0.64, biggest in short ≤50-min treatments) — all apply at A1. Do not promise conversational German; promise the four task types.

## PART A — THE B1/DTZ BUYER (condensed)

- **DTZ** (Drs. 21/5716): 2023 294,372 takers, 55.9% B1; 2024 320,343, 56.3%; 2025 ~318k, 55.0%. **~140k/year miss B1**; free 300-UE repeat hours abolished 1 Jan 2025. B1 requires B1 in **Sprechen plus one** of Hören/Lesen (33/45) or Schreiben (15/20). Section breakdowns: not published.
- **telc B1**: 300 pts, **60% in written (135/225) and oral (45/75) separately**; part-resit within 12 months; fee €155–239; counts/pass rates not published. Reddit failures cluster in Hören/Lesen/Sprachbausteine, Sprechen often full marks.
- **Goethe B1/B2**: modular, 60%/module; €259/€289 (Germany 2026); B2 failures are overwhelmingly **Schreiben**. Volumes: Goethe says "230,000 customers/yr"; per-level not found.
- **Why**: Einbürgerung B1 (§10 StAG, C1 fast track abolished 30 Oct 2025); Niederlassungserlaubnis B1, Blue Card 27→21 months with B1; nurses B2 + Fachsprachprüfung (21.5k applications 2025), doctors 12.9k. **Feb 2026 Zulassungsstopp** cut ~129,500 voluntary Integrationskurs entrants (Drs. 21/5574) → self-pay/digital.
- **Costs**: Integrationskurs €2.29/UE (€1,603), DTZ repeat €120–160, private DTZ prep €360+€120, B2 prep €1,500; tutors as above.
- **Pain**: no one corrects the Brief (ChatGPT overrates), unknown pass rules, speaking-partner lottery, listening speed, motivation collapse after failing.

## PART B — PEDAGOGY & COMPLETION (all levels)

- **Spacing**: Kim & Webb 2022 (48 experiments): medium-to-large; longer gaps win on delayed tests; optimal gap ≈10–20% of retention interval (Cepeda 2008) → exam in 6 weeks = review weekly.
- **Retrieval + feedback**: MALL meta g=0.88; retrieval apps 0.95 vs 0.73. **Corrective feedback** d=0.64 durable (Li 2010); prompts > recasts, effects largest in free production (Lyster & Saito 2010); written metalinguistic feedback strongest (d≈1.5–2.0). Immediate vs delayed timing: no measurable difference (MLJ 2023; PMC9995700) — specificity matters, not timing.
- **Extensive input**: small–medium gains, larger with accountability. **TBLT/output**: g≈0.61 (Xuan 2022 re-analysis). **Interleaving for German grammar**: not found.
- **Session length**: ≤50-min focused blocks (Li 2010), 5 days/week.
- **Completion**: MOOCs 3–6% overall, **paid/verified 46–56%** (Reich & Ruipérez-Valiente 2019). Planning prompts +29% in one study (Yeomans & Reich 2017) but **Kizilcec 2020 PNAS, 250k students: effects shrank 10×, raised week-1 activity only**. Plans naming a time of day predict completion (Cho 2024). Personalised reminders +14 pp vs generic (RCT, PMC10611998). Streaks/cohorts/first-session-win: **no rigorous RCT found** — vendor claims only.
- **Exam-prep structure (IELTS/TOEFL analogues — Magoosh, oneielts, englishmocks)**: day-1 diagnostic mock → backward plan from test date → weight weakest skill 60–70% with 20–30% maintenance → mock every 7–14 days, 2–3/week in the last 3 weeks, half-mock 48 h before, no new material in the last week → error log + rubric self-scoring; 1.25–1.5 h/day, 5 days, one rest day.

## PART C — DESIGN BRIEF (€49 self-paced A1; B1 differences noted)

**Day 1**
1. Ask purpose and **date** first (visa appointment / exam slot); everything is planned backward from it. Offer the real Goethe/telc fee and slot-scarcity facts so the date is booked early.
2. A 25-minute **diagnostic** in the four real task types, scored on the 60/100 rule; even a zero-knowledge learner gets a number and a plan — that is the first-session win.
3. One screen on the exam rules (no per-section minimum at A1; Sprechen gate at DTZ; 60%-per-part at telc) — the facts learners say they didn't know.
4. Time-of-day commitment prompt (the one planning tactic that predicts completion).

**Week 1**
5. Fixed daily 25–40-min sessions: alphabet/numbers/spelling, self-introduction recorded and scored by day 3, form-filling, 10 question frames, spaced retrieval of the **official A1 Wortliste**.
6. First 30-word note with explicit rubric feedback (Anrede, 3 points, Gruß, verb position).
7. Personalised reminder e-mails naming the learner's weakest section and days-to-exam.

**By exam date**
8. Weekly timed mock (Goethe model sets), skill-by-skill trend vs the pass line; last 3 weeks: 2 mocks/week; 48-h half-mock; no new material in the final week.
9. Speaking: AI group-exam simulation for Teile 1–3 with word/picture cards and varied accents; **Bitten** drill (Können Sie…/Kannst du…, reaction phrases).
10. Listening: the exact genres (announcement once, dialogue twice, phone message) at native speed, keyword-first strategy.
11. Error log resurfaced by spacing; ≥8 corrected notes, ≥8 recorded orals.
12. Exam-day logistics (registration cut-off, ID, certificate validity window for the visa interview).

**Non-negotiable vs nice-to-have**
13. Non-negotiable: dated backward plan, diagnostic, official word list with spaced retrieval, rubric feedback on the note and the oral, real-format mocks, personalised reminders, mobile-first and cheap-bandwidth (buyers are in Turkey, India, Kosovo, Pakistan). Nice-to-have: streaks, badges, cohorts, community.
14. **Post-fail path**: 35–38% of spouse candidates fail; sell a "retake in 4 weeks" re-plan targeting the weakest two sections, not a new course. At B1 build telc part-resit logic in.
15. **Never promise**: guaranteed pass, "A1 in 2 weeks", that the course certificate counts for any visa or Einbürgerung (only Goethe/telc/ÖSD/DTZ do; online-only certificates are rejected), pass rates or learner counts you haven't measured, or B2 nursing recognition without the Fachsprachprüfung caveat.
