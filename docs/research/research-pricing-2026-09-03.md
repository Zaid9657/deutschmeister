## 2. Packaging patterns that convert

- **Per level, lifetime, one-time** is the creator norm (Anja €347, Your German Teacher $119–189 per half level, Ankommo €9.99). "Per exam" exists only as short live prep (€89–310) or telc-trainer's €14.99 lifetime.
- **Bundles** discount 25–43 %: lingoni lifetime "~~399€~~ 229€" (−43 %), 12-mo "~~133€~~ 89€" (−33 %); Your German Teacher "save 25 %"; Babbel Lifetime "€299.99 → €199" (−33 %).
- **Lifetime vs term:** Udemy and creators = "Full Lifetime Access"; institutions cap at 3 (Goethe) or 12 months (Deutschable).
- **Course + coaching:** 2–3× the base — Marija €19 vs €59/mo with weekly Zoom; Goethe €299 self-study vs €699 tutored; Sprachcaffe group €190 vs 1:1 €330; MPU-Wissen (German one-off course) €249 vs €459.
- **Guarantees:** Udemy 30-day; Anja "30-Day No Questions Asked"; Babbel 20-day, first purchase, not App Store; Ankommo 7-day on Course Pass; Lingoda 14-day, full refund only if no credits used; DeutschAkademie "Mit Geld-zurück-Garantie" (terms not on card).
- **Payment plans:** Anja "€347 one-time or 3 × €132"; MPU-Wissen "249 € einmalig oder 3 × 83 €" (Klarna); katjagomez.de Klarna 2–6 months.
- **Five exact checkout promises:** (1) Udemy: "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee · Full Lifetime Access". (2) Ankommo: "€9.99 once · Per level, no expiry · One full level forever · 7-day money-back guarantee". (3) happygerman.co: "€347 one-time or 3 × €132 payment plan · 30-day money-back guarantee on every course". (4) lingoni: "Lifetime ~~399€~~ 229€ · Lifelong Access". (5) telc-trainer.de: "Lebenslanger Zugang – kein Abo · Einmalig, kein Abo".
- **Widerruf mechanics:** Ankommo's terms show the standard: immediate delivery + express consent → statutory withdrawal waived (§356 Abs. 5 BGB), voluntary guarantee on top.

## 4. The €49 / €129 question — and A1 vs free Nicos Weg

- **Position:** €49 per band sits between apps and creators: 5× Ankommo, 3× telc-trainer, 3–4× a Udemy sale, ⅓ of Goethe self-study (€149 promo), ⅐ of Anja, below every live prep course (€89+) and every exam fee. For a learner who already pays €155–259 for the exam it is **mid-market**; against app anchors it reads dear unless counted content is visible.
- **A1 is the hardest level to charge for.** DW Nicos Weg A1 is free, has 76 lessons, thousands of exercises and a certificate; Udemy A1 sells at $14; Ankommo A1 at €9.99. Nobody but Anja (€347) and Goethe (€149–299) charges more than €15 for A1 self-study, and both wrap it in a brand or a teacher. €49 for A1 is credible **only** if the page shows what Nicos Weg lacks: an exam-format A1 mock (Start Deutsch 1 / telc A1) with scoring, structured listening at native speed, reading/writing tasks mirroring the exam, vocabulary lists per exam topic, and a visible "for the Ehegattennachzug / Goethe A1 on <date>" framing. Without a mock exam, A1 should launch at €29–39 or be the free funnel; note the existing free-tier already exposes A1.1.
- **Bundle depth:** €129 for four (−34 %) matches lingoni/Babbel (−33 to −43 %). But B1/DTZ candidates buy A2+B1, not A1–B2: add a **€79 A2+B1 pair** as the featured card; keep €129 as ladder top.
- **€39/€59/€79:** no A/B evidence found. Observed steps: €9.99–14.99 (apps), €49–89 (lingoni term; `pricing.js` telc B1 Komplett €89), €149 (Goethe promo), €347 (Anja). €59–79 per band is defensible only with a graded mock exam and writing feedback; €39 drifts into the app tier.

## 5. Claims to avoid (UWG) and honest guarantees

- **Outcome promises:** OLG Hamm 29.01.2013 (4 U 171/12): "garantieren wir … den Lernerfolg" is irreführend (§5 UWG) — same for "Bestehen garantiert", "A1 in 4 Wochen". Lingoda hedges with "bis zu 1 GER-Stufe"; MPU-Wissen's "bestanden oder Geld zurück" is a refund condition, the only lawful shape.
- **Strike prices:** §11 PAngV (lowest price of last 30 days); LG Wiesbaden 24.04.2025 (11 O 1/25): stale reference price is irreführend. Udemy's perpetual "−84 %" and lingoni's permanent "~~399€~~" are what to avoid.
- **Guarantee wording:** BGH 19.03.2014 (I ZR 185/12): marketing the statutory Widerruf as a "Geld-zurück-Garantie" is per se unlawful (Anhang Nr. 10). A guarantee must exceed the law and its conditions must be linked from the ad (BGH I ZR 194/06). Since the Widerruf is waived on instant delivery, a voluntary 30-day refund is a real value-add — say so.
- **Reviews/counts:** Anhang Nr. 23b/23c + §5b Abs. 3 — "Kundenbewertungen" only with verification and disclosure; usage counts stay banned by the repo rule.
- **FernUSG:** BGH III ZR 109/24 (12.06.2025) and III ZR 173/24 (02.10.2025): a paid, mostly-asynchronous video course with **any contractual right to individual questions/feedback** is Fernunterricht → ZFU-Zulassung or the contract is void and fully refundable. Pure self-study without Lernerfolgskontrolle is exempt. Do not bundle human feedback or a "Fragen-Recht" into the €49 course; describe Pro's AI feedback as an automated tool.
- **Standard and honest:** statutory 14-day Widerruf (mention, don't market); voluntary "30 Tage Geld zurück, ohne Angabe von Gründen" with linked terms; "lebenslanger Zugang" defined as product lifetime; "Stand:"-dated competitor figures.

## 6. Recommendation

- **Price:** €49 per band for A2/B1/B2. For **A1 launch: €49 only with an A1 mock exam shipped; otherwise €29** (still 3× Ankommo) and raise to €49 when the mock lands, documenting the price history for §11 PAngV. Featured card: €79 A2+B1. Ladder top: €129 all four (−34 %).
- **Access:** lifetime, instant delivery with §356 Abs. 5 consent at checkout; no App Store.
- **Guarantee:** "30 Tage Geld zurück, ohne Angabe von Gründen", terms linked beside the badge; no pass promise anywhere.
- **Pro (€9.99/mo):** unchanged; course buyers keep the existing 90-day Pro window (`COURSE_PRO_DAYS`) as the bridge, worded as automated tools (FernUSG).
- **Three "what you get" lines** — each derivable from `src/data/marketing.js`; verify the per-band split before publishing:
  1. "16 Grammatikthemen mit ~168 Übungen auf A1" — true if `GRAMMAR_TOPIC_COUNT` 64 and `GRAMMAR_EXERCISE_COUNT` 672 split evenly over 8 sub-levels; needs a per-level count query.
  2. "16 Lesetexte + 12 Hörübungen mit Muttersprachler-Audio" — from `READING_LESSON_COUNTS_BY_LEVEL` (8+8 for A1) and `LISTENING_EXERCISE_COUNT` (6 per sub-level).
  3. "1 vollständige Modellprüfung Start Deutsch 1 / telc A1 mit Auswertung" — **not true today**: mock exams exist only for Goethe B1, DTZ, telc B2 (PR #40). An A1 mock must ship before this line, and it is the line that separates €49 from free Nicos Weg.

If those numbers are not on the checkout page, €49 for A1 reads as an app price without an app; the counted numbers are the product.
