**The north star is now 1,000,000 US dollars in DeutschMeister sales per month. Signups, Telegram,
traffic and course quality are means, not the goal.**

- The plan, with the math: [docs/strategy/million-plan-2026-10.md](../docs/strategy/million-plan-2026-10.md).

## Rules that follow from this

1. **Every task states, in one line, which sales number it should move** (and by roughly how much).
   A task that moves no sales number is cut or parked.
2. **Distribution and conversion come before polish.** Getting in front of buyers and turning them
   into payers beats refining what few people see.
3. **The simplest version that can sell ships first.** Manual before automated, one test before a
   batch, one sale before a system.

## Memory and method (every session)

- **Before any business decision:** read the Notion page "DeutschMeister — Shared Board" (Notion
  search). If Notion is not connected, read `.planning/method.md` and `.planning/lessons.md`
  instead, and say so.
- **Update the board in the same step** as any of these: (1) the founder makes, changes or reverses
  a decision; (2) something proves false or a lesson is learned; (3) a stage passes or starts;
  (4) a deliverable is saved; (5) money, credits, tools or access change. Before ending a reply,
  the board must be newer than the last commit that carries a decision or lesson.
- Lessons go to the board and to `.planning/lessons.md` (newest first).
- One decision per message to the founder; state each newly locked decision to him in one line.
